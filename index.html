LuxeDesk — Guide for Cleaning Businesses & Fitness Trainers

Recurring bookings, clear client records, and invoices that get paid — a practical guide for cleaning businesses and fitness trainers.

Introduction

Cleaning businesses and fitness trainers share the same challenge: regular clients, repeating appointments, and getting paid on time. A weekly home clean, a fortnightly office clean, or a standing one-to-one training slot all depend on the same habits — keeping good client records, confirming the next visit, and sending a clear invoice. This guide covers the practical steps that keep those routines running smoothly, and how LuxeDesk brings them together in one app.

Client tips

  • Keep one record per client with contact details and a short note — for cleaners, that means access instructions, keys, and preferences; for trainers, goals, limitations, and session history.
  • Review client notes before every appointment and update them afterwards, so nothing important lives only in chat messages.
  • Record payment terms agreed with each client before the first booking, and record every payment on the day it arrives.
  • Use intake forms to collect the information you need from new clients before the first visit or session, not after it.

Booking tips

  • Book recurring clients as a repeating block, not one session at a time, so the slot is protected week after week.
  • Confirm the next visit and remind clients the day before — a short reminder prevents missed visits and empty training slots.
  • Review recurring bookings monthly: renew, pause, or adjust blocks that have ended.
  • When a booking changes, update the calendar straight away; a change agreed verbally and never written down is a missed visit waiting to happen.
  • Use a shareable booking page so new clients can book a first appointment without a long exchange of messages.

Invoice tips

  • Send invoices the same day as the work — cleaners after each clean, trainers when a session package renews.
  • Use branded invoice templates for regular work, one-off jobs, and session packages, so every invoice looks professional and matches your business.
  • Review overdue invoices once a week and follow up promptly; sending invoices late and forgetting which ones are unpaid is one of the most common mistakes.
  • Track income, invoices, and payments alongside bookings, and review your finances monthly: invoiced, received, and outstanding.

How LuxeDesk helps
